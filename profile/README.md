## The art of making

**TeXne / τέχνη**

The name **TeXne** is a modern construction inspired by *techne* (τέχνη) — an ancient Greek concept associated with art, craft, skill and practical know-how.

At the heart of the idea is something simple: **thinking and making are not separate acts.** Design, judgement, technique and execution belong to the same creative process.

**Tezshouse** represents imagination, personality, ideas and creative instinct.  
**TeXne** represents craft, skill, technique and the practical ability to turn those ideas into something real.

Together, they represent a way of making where creativity and execution belong to the same process.

**Inspect. Clean. Verify.**

[MetaTaken](https://github.com/Tezshouse-TeXne/MetaTaken) is a free, local Windows utility for inspecting, cleaning and verifying image metadata — without cloud image processing.

It is built around a deliberately simple workflow: drop in an image, inspect what is there, clean what can be safely removed, and automatically verify the result.
